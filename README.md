## About the business

- **What it does:** Nouveau Labs provides software product engineering, technology development and enterprise transformation services, alongside its own product and platform initiatives.
- **Who it works with:** technology companies, start-ups and enterprise teams developing products, integrating business systems or modernising operations.
- **Where it operates:** the company's public profile lists its headquarters in Bengaluru and a US presence in California. Its official company description identifies product development and support as core work.
- **What makes the offer complex:** different customers arrive looking for AI, communications, networking, security, platform development or enterprise workflow solutions. Those capabilities overlap but should still be distinguishable.
- **Why the website matters:** B2B buyers need evidence of relevant capabilities and a clear contact route without having to decode every technical acronym first.

Nouveau Labs' published business information covers artificial intelligence, machine learning, analytics, automation, robotics, cloud, wireless networking, unified communications, embedded systems and mobility. Its [Odoo partner listing](https://www.odoo.com/partners/nouveau-labs-private-limited-1799375?country_id=101&grade_id=3) also identifies ERP and business intelligence work.

## The problem

A business with numerous enterprise services and proprietary products can become difficult to understand online. Visitors may arrive from quite different starting points: a product manager looking for an engineering partner, an IT leader researching network infrastructure, or a business operations team evaluating AI-assisted workflows.

When those audiences share one website, the risk is **burying important capabilities under a large number of links**. A useful enterprise site needs clear service families, a separate product route, credible company information and a direct path to discuss a relevant requirement.

This is the communication challenge evident in Nouveau Labs' published offering and the saved website design files. No documented audit of its previous website or pre-project conversion figures was available.

## How the website project was approached

The items below are grounded in the Tech Conversion portfolio description and **saved Nouveau Labs component files**. The files show design and implementation-stage work, but do not independently prove that every element is still live on the production website.

1. **Separate services from products.** A saved *Capability Explorer* component provides two main choices—Services and Products—so buyers do not have to interpret them as the same kind of offering.
2. **Group technical services into recognisable families.** The component organises AI Solutions, Cyber Security, IP Networking, Unified Communications and CRM/ERP capabilities into separate tabs with corresponding explanations.
3. **Give products their own discovery route.** The saved product tabs include AskAI, DolphinCX, WhatsApp Connector and In-App Calling. Their presence in project materials should not be mistaken for verification of every product's current availability or feature set.
4. **Support deeper technical exploration.** Component drafts contain links to specific service topics and context-aware consultation actions, helping a visitor move from a capability overview to a more detailed discussion.
5. **Improve complex footer navigation.** The supplied before-and-after materials and project visuals show grouped Services, Products, Company and Contact columns, helping the footer function as a secondary sitemap rather than a miscellaneous link list.
6. **Give enterprise enquiries a clear action.** A saved footer variation includes a consultation banner and supporting calls to action. Enquiry handling and CRM delivery were not independently tested.
7. **Account for smaller screens.** Responsive UI work demonstrates that narrower layouts were considered. Formal mobile usability and accessibility test results were not available.
8. **Address website housekeeping.** A saved cookie-policy component indicates content work related to site notices, but it is not proof of regulatory compliance or a verified live consent implementation.

## Standards the website should be reviewed against

These are **evaluation references**, not claims that Nouveau Labs' website passed a formal audit or that client infrastructure holds a particular certification.

- [Core Web Vitals](https://web.dev/articles/vitals): assess loading, interaction responsiveness and visual stability across desktop and mobile.
- [WCAG 2.2, Level AA](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/): review accessible service tabs, keyboard operation, focus order, labels and contrast.
- [Google Search Essentials](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials): evaluate service-page discoverability, useful content and indexability.
- [Schema.org](https://schema.org/): check accurate organisation, breadcrumb and service markup where implemented.
- [India's Digital Personal Data Protection framework](https://www.meity.gov.in/data-protection-framework): review applicable handling of business enquiries, recruitment data and cookie choices.
- **Enterprise claim accuracy:** verify security, uptime, compliance, AI-performance and customer-adoption claims before publishing figures or certification badges.
